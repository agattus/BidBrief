Vrinda was a woman of unmatched purity and an absolute devotee of Lord Vishnu. Her devotion and loyalty created a mystical, impenetrable shield around her husband. As long as Vrinda remained faithful and her chastity was unbroken, Jalandhara was literally immortal. No weapon forged in the cosmos could leave a single scratch on his skin.

The battle was catastrophic. The universe shook as the Creator of Destruction fought the byproduct of his own anger. But as Shiva's Trishul struck Jalandhara, the Gods watched in pure horror. The weapon bounced off. Because of Vrinda's spiritual shield, even the Supreme Destroyer could not kill him. The cosmos was on the brink of total annihilation.
