# Character bible (lock for this episode)

## Vrinda
- Age: young woman, early 20s
- Face: youthful, clear skin, dark eyes, red bindi
- Hair: long dark wavy hair
- Costume: cream / off-white saree with gold embroidery
- Jewelry: gold jhumkas, layered gold necklaces, bangles
- Never elderly, never grey hair, never heavy wrinkles

## Jalandhara
- Charcoal / volcanic dark skin (not soft blue-grey)
- Shiva-like face: thin mustache, third-eye / tripundra mark
- Dark demonic black-gold armor, spiked jeweled crown
- Glowing amber/fire eyes when powered

## Shiva
- Ash-blue skin, jata with crescent moon, rudraksha, tiger skin
- Trishul, third eye

## Vishnu
- Light blue skin, golden mukut with peacock feather
- Yellow pitambar, serene / sorrowful when accepting curse
